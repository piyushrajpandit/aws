3. PREAMBLE The following specification describes the nature of the invention:

4. FIELD OF THE INVENTION

The present invention relates to computer vision, distributed web crawling architectures, and automated consumer rights enforcement systems. More specifically, the present invention relates to a computer-implemented system that performs multi-pass optical character recognition (OCR), automated statutory compliance auditing under Rule 6(10) of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, real-time spatial detection of Dual Maximum Retail Price (Dual-MRP) extortion under Section 36(1) of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009, and personalized biochemical health hazard mitigation.

5. BACKGROUND AND PRIOR ART DEFICIENCIES

Under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, all packaged goods sold physically or online in India must carry mandatory declarations: manufacturer identity (Rule 6(1)(a)), country of origin (Rule 6(1)(b)), net metric quantity (Rule 6(1)(d)), inclusive MRP (Rule 6(1)(f)), and consumer grievance contacts (Rule 6(1)(g)).

Deficiencies in Existing Art:

  • Absence of Automated Digital Oversight: Over 25,000 marketplace listings breach Rule 6(10) daily without regulatory detection due to manual inspection bottlenecks.
  • Undetected Dual-MRP Fraud: Unscrupulous retail operators in transit corridors charge above statutory MRP without real-time consumer verification tools.
  • Anti-Bot Web Scraper Brittleness: Marketplace anti-bot defenses (HTTP 429) cause traditional scrapers to crash or return empty product metadata.

6. SUMMARY OF THE TECHNICAL INVENTION

The present invention overcomes the aforesaid limitations by providing:

  1. Resilient Semantic Slug Reconstructor: When an anti-bot HTTP 429 surge response is received, the crawler strips tracking bloat and decodes the canonical URL slug, identifying brand, metric unit (e.g., 10 kg, 500 g), and statutory entity profiles without manual intervention.
  2. Codified Statutory Rule Engine: Mathematically verifies listed prices against Unit Sale Price (USP) and calculates Section 36(1) statutory compounding penalties (₹25,000 to ₹1,50,000).
  3. Geo-Spatial Dual-MRP Evidence Logger: Cross-references physical shelf prices with baseline databases and auto-dispatches geo-tagged violations to state supervisors.
  4. Personalized Health Risk Interceptor: Cross-references packaging ingredient OCR with patient medical profiles (e.g., Type-2 Diabetes) to flag concealed glycemic hazards.

7. NOVEL AND INVENTIVE CLAIMS

  1. An autonomous software system configured to audit digital marketplace listings against statutory legal metrology mandates in real time.
  2. A resilient anti-bot bypass mechanism extracting metric packaging parameters directly from marketplace URL slugs upon receiving HTTP 429 rate-limiting.
  3. An automated legal engine formulating Section 36(1) Show-Cause notices populated with compounding fine calculations.
