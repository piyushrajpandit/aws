# 🎯 SatyaDrishti — Problem Statement

---

## Problem 1: The ₹10 Scam Nobody Fights

A company sells a product for ₹10 — wrong MRP printed, missing manufacturer details, incorrect net quantity.

One consumer notices something is off. But ₹10 is too small to fight over. They move on.

The company knows this. So they sell the **same fraudulent product 10,000 times.** Across 100 cities. To lakhs of people. The total fraud? **Crores of rupees — earned legally, because nobody reported it.**

**The system has no way to connect individual suspicions into collective evidence.**

SatyaDrishti changes this. Every time a user scans a product and flags it, that report doesn't disappear — it feeds into a supervisor's live intelligence dashboard. When 50 people report the same brand from the same city, the inspector knows exactly **where to go tomorrow morning.**

> **Individual complaints are ignored. Collective data becomes an FIR.**

---

## Problem 2: 400 Million People Eating the Wrong Food

India has **101 million diabetics** and **315 million people with hypertension.** Their doctor says avoid sugar, avoid sodium — but nobody tells them which exact products on the shelf are safe.

They stand in a grocery store. They pick up a packet. The label is in English. The numbers mean nothing to them. They guess. They buy. They go home.

**And then the wrong food silently makes their condition worse — not because they didn't care, but because they had no tool at the point of decision.**

SatyaDrishti solves this. A user scans any product. The app reads the actual nutrition panel from the label. It checks against their personal profile — diabetes, BP, fitness goal, dietary preference. It gives a simple verdict:

> ✅ **Safe for you** — or — ❌ **Avoid. High sugar. Try this instead.**

No guessing. No medical degree required. The right decision, in 3 seconds, at the point of purchase.
